# Community

Where VTES communities actually live today — the ground a new platform competes on. Observed
2026-09-26 from the public pages cited, no logins. Counts are forum-reported totals; online/"record"
visitor counts are bot-inflated and not a measure of anything.

## National forums

**France — [vekn.fr/forum](https://www.vekn.fr/forum/)** — phpBB, local accounts. 169k posts, 13.5k
topics, 1,359 members. Boards: game, beginners, rules, tournaments, trading, an English international
section, the association (loi 1901) board, archives, off-topic. Regional category "Domaines de
France" with six regions, Paris/IdF by far the largest; no city or playgroup boards. Live boards on
the survey date: tournaments and trading (same-day posts), game, rules, association. A separate
[Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/vtesfrance/) exists.

**Germany — [forum.vekn.de](https://forum.vekn.de/)** — phpBB, local accounts. 61k posts, 6.1k topics,
245 members. Category "Spielegruppen" has **one board per city or region** — Rhein-Main (1k topics,
active), Bonn/Köln and Bochum/Herne (active), Hamburg, Freiburg, Fulda, Berlin (thin), Stuttgart,
Braunschweig, Magdeburg (dormant since 2022–24). A [links thread](https://forum.vekn.de/viewtopic.php?t=56241)
points to the official Discord, archon, r/VTES and four Facebook groups.

## International forum

**[vekn.net/forum](https://www.vekn.net/forum/index)** — Kunena on Joomla. 115k messages, 15k topics,
10.8k users (spam accounts among the newest). **No national sub-forums**: one "City, State, Country &
Region Talk" board, event announcements split by continent, two dormant language boards. Live: event
reports & TWD, rules questions, news, general discussion. Trading and online-play boards are dead.

## Chat-first communities

- **Official Discord** ([Black Chantry](https://discord.com/servers/vampire-the-eternal-struggle-official-887471681277399091)),
  3.2k members, language sections by role, advertises finding local events and playgroups.
- **Spain/Portugal** ([vtes.es](https://vtes.es/es/)) — website + Discord (their general assembly
  meets there), Facebook, Instagram. No forum.
- **Italy** ([vtesitaly.com](https://vtesitaly.com/en/community/)) — website + Facebook, WhatsApp,
  Telegram, Instagram. No forum.
- **Czechia** ([vtes.cz](https://www.vtes.cz/en/)) — "most activity happens on Discord".
- **Brazil** — WhatsApp for trading; no forum found.
- Many older national and city sites listed on [vekn.net](https://www.vekn.net/other-v-tes-websites)
  are dead or static; NYC and Cape Town use Meetup.

## What follows

- Forums stay the durable home for tournament announcements and reports, rules archives and trading
  where they still exist (France, Germany). Day-to-day chat has moved to Discord or WhatsApp/Telegram.
- The German playgroup boards already match the NC → Prince → playgroup shape; activity is heavily
  concentrated (Paris, Rhein-Main) and most local boards go dormant.
- Every forum runs its own account silo; none ties into archon.
- Newer associations skipped forums entirely: for them a new platform competes with chat apps, not
  with an old forum.
